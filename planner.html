    Import from Degree Works

    1. Drag this button to your browser's bookmarks bar (one-time setup):
      Send to Planner
      Drag doesn't work? , then right-click the bookmarks bar → Add page…, name it Send to Planner, and paste into URL.
    2. Open Degree Works and sign in as usual. Open your degree audit.
    3. Click Send to Planner in your bookmarks bar. This planner opens in a new tab with your courses and requirements.

    Adding a minor or certificate? In Degree Works, run a What-If audit for it, then click Send to Planner again. The new program is added next to your others.

    Your record is copied straight from the Degree Works tab into this one. It is stored only in this browser and is never sent to a server. On a shared computer, use Plans ▾ → Delete when you're done.
